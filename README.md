Kio is a little pixel cat I made to sit on my desktop.

I wanted to make something simple that could walk around my screen and just feel like a small character living on my desktop.

## What Kio does

* Walks around
*  Sits and idles
*  Runs
* Jumps
* Can be dragged with the mouse
* Stays on top of other windows
* Has different pixel animations

## Why I made it

I just wanted a little cat on my desktop.

I also wanted to learn more about Godot and how desktop-style applications work.

The animation part was probably the most annoying part. I had to draw the frames and make sure the movement between them didn't look weird. Since the cat is pixel art, even changing a few pixels can make the animation look different.

I kept adding things whenever I got an idea, which is how Kio ended up with walking, running, jumping and dragging.

## Project

The project is made with Godot and is mainly focused on the desktop pet itself.

I'm still working on it and I want to add more animations and small things to make Kio feel more alive.
